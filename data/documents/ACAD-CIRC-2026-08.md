### 1. Supersession

1.1 This circular **supersedes clause R.1(B).22** of the Academic Programmes
Ordinance and Regulations 2022 (ACAD-ORD-2022) with immediate effect.

1.2 All other provisions of ACAD-ORD-2022 remain in force.

### 2. Minimum attendance

2.1 With effect from the date of this circular, a student of a UG programme
must have a **minimum attendance of 80%** of the total number of classes
including lectures, tutorials and practicals held in a subject, in order to be
eligible to appear at the End-Term Examination for that subject.

2.2 The relaxation of attendance provided for in clause R.1(B).22 of
ACAD-ORD-2022 may continue to be granted by the Dean for reasons to be recorded
in writing, subject to clause 2.1 above.

### 3. Examination and passing

3.1 A student must obtain a **minimum of 40%** of the total marks in a course
in order to be declared to have passed that course.

3.2 A student whose result in a course is **FAIL or ABSENT** may register for
the supplementary examination for that course. A student who has been
**DETAINED** for shortage of attendance is not eligible to register for the
supplementary examination and must repeat the course.

### 4. Placement eligibility

4.1 A student must have a **CGPA of at least 6.0** to register for campus
placement.

4.2 A student must have **no active backlogs** at the time of registration for
campus placement.
